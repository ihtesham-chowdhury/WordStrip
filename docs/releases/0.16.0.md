# WordStrip 0.16.0

Eight themes, two of them new, and the monospaced one finally monospaced. **Nothing here changes what
WordStrip predicts, what it inserts, or how keys are routed.** Your settings carry over.

## Downloads

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `WordStrip-Setup-0.16.0.exe` | Installer, with Start Menu entries and an uninstaller. Recommended. |
| `WordStrip-0.16.0-portable.zip` | A single self-contained program. Unzip it and run `WordStrip.exe`. |

SHA-256 sums are published with the release assets; a rebuild changes them, so they are not recorded here.

## Fixed: the Terminal theme was never monospaced

The bar's layout had the font written into it, so whatever a theme asked for was ignored and everything
rendered in Segoe UI. Terminal now really is Cascadia Code (or Cascadia Mono, or Consolas), and its columns
are a little wider to suit monospaced letters — "forward to" no longer gets shortened in a column it fits.

## Two new themes

**Prism Rail.** No panel at all: the words sit on your page, and the only drawn thing is a hairline rail
underneath, lit from its left end to a dot under the selected word. The lit length tells you where in the
list you are, which nothing else here does. A barely-there wash keeps the words readable over photographs
and busy pages.

**Editorial.** A ruled ivory column — thin dark border, a rule between every candidate, square corners, and
a heavy ink underline under the selected word. Flat by conviction: no blur, no gloss, no elevation.

**Paper & Ink** has been retuned as its opposite so the two do not overlap: warm, unruled, softer, with a
rust-coloured mark and a whisper of shadow. One is a page; the other is a set column.

## Three themes rebuilt to the reference designs

- **Command** is now the dense dark instrument: a deep slab, the selected word raised a shade out of it, and
  a short coral rule beneath.
- **Fluent Surface** takes a filled accent pill with white text — which is both what the reference showed and
  what a Windows text selection looks like.
- **Spatial Glass** is the other way up: white luminous material with a soft grey capsule, rather than white
  on white, with its roundness and breathing room back.

## Still true

- The bar sizes itself to the text you are writing (Automatic by default; Compact, Standard and Comfortable
  are fixed).
- Every pair of themes is checked automatically for being genuinely different — including with the colour
  removed. All 28 pairs pass.

## Known limitations

- Dark mode, High Contrast, transparency-off and display scales other than 150% are still unverified on real
  hardware.
- Terminal falls back to Consolas if you do not have Cascadia installed. It only ever changes the bar, never
  the application you are typing in.
- English only.
