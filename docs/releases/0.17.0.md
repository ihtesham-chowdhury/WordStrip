# WordStrip 0.17.0

Fixes from a long session of real use: the bar no longer drifts while you type, and the four themes that were
wrong in practice have been rebuilt. **Nothing here changes what WordStrip predicts or inserts.**

## Downloads

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `WordStrip-Setup-0.17.0.exe` | Installer, with Start Menu entries and an uninstaller. Recommended. |
| `WordStrip-0.17.0-portable.zip` | A single self-contained program. Unzip it and run `WordStrip.exe`. |

SHA-256 sums are published with the release assets; a rebuild changes them, so they are not recorded here.

## Fixed: the bar quietly changing opacity and colour

Real bug, now fixed. WordStrip samples the screen behind the bar whenever you pause, so its material can stand
off whatever is back there. No two samples of a page you are typing into are the same — a line scrolls, a
cursor blinks, a page repaints a shade lighter — and the bar was recomputing its surface for every one of
those tiny differences. Over a long session that reads as exactly what you saw.

The measurement is now settled into a few wide bands, and the material is rebuilt only when the backdrop has
genuinely changed. Within a band, nothing moves at all.

## Spatial Glass is now actually glass

A brighter frosted body with a lit top edge, a lensed rim, the widest corners in the set and a wide soft
shadow that lifts it off the page.

**Its dark version is fixed.** It used to be a pale veil with dark text, which over a dark application looked
like tracing paper dropped on the screen. Dark glass is dark: a deep translucent body, light type, and a
selection that is a brighter piece of the same material.

## Editorial and Paper & Ink pulled apart

They were too close to justify both.

- **Editorial** moves to cooler paper stock with a stronger black keyline, tighter rhythm and lighter type —
  a set column.
- **Paper & Ink** goes further the other way: warmer, rounder, no rules at all, a rust pencil mark — a page.
- **Editorial's dark version** is a night edition of the same column, deep ink with ivory type and ivory
  rules, rather than an inverted page.

## Command has a light version

As suggested. Over a dark editor it is the near-black instrument it was. Over a white page it is the same
design in daylight: a cool grey slab with a white raised key, because a near-black bar over a white document
is a hole cut in the page.

## Terminal is no longer a hole in the page either

Over a dark application it stays the OLED black a terminal is. Over a white page it moves to graphite —
unmistakably still a terminal, without the hard edge of pure black against paper.

## Known limitations

- Dark mode, High Contrast, transparency-off and display scales other than 150% are still unverified on real
  hardware.
- Two themes deliberately depend on the automatic contrast floor to stay readable: Prism Rail, which is
  meant to be barely there, and Terminal's dark version over a black editor.
- English only.
