# WordStrip 0.14.0

The visual polish release. **Nothing here changes what WordStrip predicts, what it inserts, or how keys are routed** — the engine and the interaction model were deliberately locked for this work. Everything below is how it looks and where it sits.

Upgrading from 0.13.3 keeps all your settings.

## Downloads

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `WordStrip-Setup-0.14.0.exe` | Installer, with Start Menu entries and an uninstaller. Recommended. |
| `WordStrip-0.14.0-portable.zip` | A single self-contained program. Unzip it and run `WordStrip.exe`. Nothing needs installing. |

SHA-256 sums are published with the release assets; a rebuild changes them, so they are not recorded here.

## The bar

**Two clear states.** While you type, the strip is information: the first word reads a little stronger, the rest sit quietly behind it. When Space will finish the word, that word sits in a highlight — present, but calm. When you press Tab, the same highlight comes up to full strength with its position marker underneath. You can tell at a glance whether you are reading suggestions or driving them.

**Nothing moves when it shouldn't.** A word's weight now depends on which slot it is in, never on whether it is selected, so choosing a suggestion no longer re-measures the word and nudges it inside its column.

**Quieter dividers.** The lines between columns group them without being read as punctuation between the words.

**It follows the screen you are typing on.** Placement is now worked out in the physical pixels of whichever display holds your cursor — previously it used the primary display's dimensions and scale, which put the bar on the wrong screen, or the right screen at the wrong size, on a multi-monitor setup. Positions land on whole pixels, so edges and text stay crisp.

**It never covers the line you are writing.** Following the cursor places the bar below your line, and above it when there is no room below.

**It stays visible over anything.** Every theme now keeps a minimum contrast against whatever is behind it. Before this, a pale theme over a white page was, arithmetically, invisible — nine of the fourteen theme variants failed that test. All seven themes were re-drawn to pass it on their own.

**All seven themes tuned individually:** Fluent Acrylic and Apple Frosted lost their showier glass; Mica is now the quietest, with the selection doing the work; Fluent 2 + Depth is less decorative rather than heavier; Raycast is a deep neutral surface instead of black on black; visionOS is far less see-through over dark apps, where text behind it used to read straight through the bar; Material 3 keeps its tonal lilac with lighter elevation.

## Settings

**Seven pages behind a navigation rail** — Appearance, Suggestions, Your words, Learning & privacy, Context model, Integrations, General — instead of one long scroll.

**No Apply, no Done.** Settings have always applied the moment you change them; the pinned footer only ever overlaid the content it sat on.

**A theme gallery.** Each theme is a tile showing a real miniature of the bar, drawn by the same code that draws the real one, over the kind of background that theme was made for.

**A preview that holds still** in its own column, showing four states at once: while you are typing and after Tab, over a light page and over a dark app.

**Real controls.** Toggle switches, segmented controls for things with a few choices (words shown, light/dark, placement, motion), proper sliders, text boxes and scrollbars. Nothing stock is left.

**Status instead of paragraphs.** The context model reads "Active", "Ready" or "Not installed" with the action beside it. Integrations says whether browser and Office support is on, then lists which applications that actually covers. Learning leads with a state and a number. Details sit behind expanders.

**Your words** shows its count, reveals Remove on the row you point at, and tells you what to do when the list is empty.

**It follows Windows.** Light, dark and High Contrast, including the title bar.

## Accessibility

- Keyboard-navigable throughout, with one visible focus ring on every control.
- Reduced motion is respected everywhere: the last animation that ignored it — the bar's hover fade — is gone.
- High Contrast replaces the whole palette with the system's own colours.
- No status is shown by colour alone; each carries a shape and a word as well.

## Known limitations

- Dark mode, High Contrast, transparency-off and display scales other than 150% have not been tried on real hardware for this release. The code paths exist and the colour palettes are checked automatically, but if you run any of those, that is the most useful thing to look at.
- After a word that's already complete, a quick Tab stays on that word so Tab Tab can move along the strip. For a next-word suggestion, press Space then Tab.
- English only.
