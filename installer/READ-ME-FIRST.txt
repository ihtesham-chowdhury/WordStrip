WordStrip — early preview
=========================

Thanks for testing this. It adds phone-keyboard-style word suggestions and autocorrect
to the normal Windows keyboard.


WHICH FILE DO I RUN?
--------------------

  WordStrip-Setup-*.exe       Installs it properly (Start Menu entry, optional start-with-Windows,
                              uninstall from Add/Remove Programs). No admin password needed.
                              Recommended.

  portable\WordStrip.exe      No installation. Just double-click it. Delete the file to remove it.

Either one works on its own. You do NOT need to install .NET or anything else.


"WINDOWS PROTECTED YOUR PC" — THIS IS EXPECTED
---------------------------------------------

The app isn't code-signed (a certificate costs a few hundred dollars a year), so Windows
shows a blue SmartScreen warning the first time.

To run it anyway:

  1. Click "More info"
  2. Click "Run anyway"

You'll only see this once.


HOW TO USE IT
-------------

1. Start WordStrip. It has no window — it lives in the system tray (bottom-right,
   you may need to click the "^" arrow to see it).

2. First launch takes about 10-20 seconds while it builds its spelling index.
   After that it starts quickly.

3. Open Notepad and start typing. A glass bar appears with suggested words.

     Tab            highlight the next word (hold it to scrub through them quickly)
     Space          insert the highlighted word      <-- the main one
     Esc            put the bar away
     Click a word   inserts it straight away, no need to press Tab first

   Using the keyboard, you press Tab first and then Space. If nothing is highlighted,
   Space is just a normal space, so your ordinary typing is never affected.

4. NEW IN 0.5: between words, the bar now PREDICTS WHAT COMES NEXT from the words you
   just typed, instead of showing the same handful of common words every time.

   Type "how are" and it offers: you, we, they
   Type "let me"  and it offers: see, go, have, know

   It also reorders suggestions while you're typing a word. After "I am", typing "wor"
   ranks "working" above "work", even though "work" is the more common word on its own.

   This is a statistical model built from public-domain books, running entirely on your
   machine. It has three words of context and no understanding of meaning, so it's good
   at ordinary English sentences and weak on technical or work jargon.

   Click a predicted word to insert it. (Between words the bar deliberately doesn't
   capture Tab — see below.)

   The bar STAYS ON SCREEN between words rather than vanishing every time you press
   space, the way a phone keyboard's suggestion row does.

   While it's just sitting there between words it doesn't take over any keys — Tab
   still indents and moves between fields, Esc still closes dialogs. It only takes
   over Tab and Space once you're part-way through typing a word and it's offering
   you completions.

   It goes away when you click somewhere else, press Esc, or leave the text box.

   If you preferred the old behaviour, there's a switch for it:
   Settings -> Suggestions -> "Keep the bar on screen between words".

5. Autocorrect fixes obvious misspellings when you finish a word
   ("recieve" becomes "receive").

6. Right-click the tray icon -> Settings. There's a live preview, and you can change:

     - whether the bar stays on screen between words
     - how many words are shown (3 to 7)
     - how thick the bar is
     - how thick/transparent the glass looks
     - how fast the animation is
     - where the bar sits: bottom of the screen, top, or following your text cursor


WHAT DOESN'T WORK YET — PLEASE DON'T REPORT THESE
-------------------------------------------------

* It only works in standard Windows text boxes: Notepad, and most desktop app text
  fields and dialogs.
* It does NOT work in Chrome, Edge, or any browser. It does not work in Electron apps
  (Slack, Discord, VS Code) or in Microsoft Office. This is the single biggest known
  gap and it's being worked on.
* English only.
* Predictions come from word statistics, not from understanding your sentence. The
  books they were built from are mostly 19th-century novels, so ordinary English is
  well covered and modern/technical phrasing is not.


PRIVACY
-------

Everything runs on your own machine. There is no internet connection, no account, and
no telemetry of any kind. Nothing you type is saved to disk or sent anywhere.

The app deliberately turns itself off inside password fields.


WHAT FEEDBACK IS USEFUL
-----------------------

* Did the suggestions actually help, or get in the way?
* Are the next-word predictions any good? Which ones were obviously wrong or silly?
* Do you WANT to reach the predicted words from the keyboard? Right now you have to
  click them, because capturing Tab between words would stop Tab indenting and moving
  between fields everywhere else. If clicking is annoying, say so and it can get its
  own shortcut.
* Does the bar staying on screen between words feel calmer than it flickering away
  after every word, or is it just clutter? Did you end up turning it off?
* Is Tab-then-Space comfortable, or would you prefer something else?
* Did text ever get inserted wrongly, or in the wrong colour/font? If so, which app?
* Does the animation feel right — too fast, too slow, or fine?
* Does the bar sit in a sensible place, or is it in the way?
* Anything that felt slow, ugly, or confusing.


TO UNINSTALL
------------

Installed version:  Settings -> Apps -> Installed apps -> WordStrip -> Uninstall
Portable version:   Right-click the tray icon -> Exit, then delete WordStrip.exe
